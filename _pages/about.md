I am Jiale Fu, a master's student at Southeast University and PALM Lab, where I work on efficient inference for large language models. My recent interests center on speculative decoding, collaborative decoding, and reasoning-oriented inference strategies that make language models faster and more reliable.

Before graduate study, I completed a bachelor's degree in mathematics at Southeast University and worked on multimodal and reasoning systems during internships at Shopee R&D Center and Baidu Research.

You can also find me via [email](mailto:jiale.fu@seu.edu.cn), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ec38OWoAAAAJ), [GitHub](https://github.com/jialefu), and [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/people/shui-ba-shui-de-shi-yan-dang-zhen-27).

### Research

- Efficient inference for large language models
- Speculative decoding and collaborative decoding
- Reasoning and in-context learning

### Experience

- **Baidu Research** - Research Intern, Nov 2023-May 2024. Focused on reasoning and in-context learning for large language models.
